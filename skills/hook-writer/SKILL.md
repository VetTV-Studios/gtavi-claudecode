# Hook writer

Write 3 options per clip. Sentence case. Short: 3 to 9 words fit the hook box. The footage is Vet's own recording. Do not name other streamers and do not write a reaction title about someone else's face.

## What works

- Number promise: "3 things in this garage"
- Shock fact: "This car was one block away"
- Situation: "When the cops take the wrong street"
- Negation: "You can't do this in GTA 5"
- Question: "Would you take this jump?"

## Titles on Vet's own facecam

For the `react` layout the title sits on the seam between his camera and the game. Describe what happens on screen. CAPS on 2 or 3 words at most.

- "The cop MISSED the alley"
- "This garage was unmarked"

Do not invent a quote. Emojis are optional, at most 2, and usually belong in the caption rather than the title.

## What does not work

- Bare labels: "GTA 6 clip", "Funny moment"
- "Wait for it", "You won't believe this", "Watch till the end"
- Fake urgency or CAPS everywhere
- Another creator's name, or a title that implies we clipped their stream

## Rules

1. The clip must deliver the hook. If the hook says 3 things, show 3. No clickbait.
2. No spoilers for story moments in launch week. Label such clips SPOILER in the caption, not in the hook.
3. Never imply the page is Rockstar. Never write "official" for fan content.
4. Use "GTA 6". One emoji at most in the hook.
5. Do not reuse the same hook within a week. Check `log.md`.
6. Facts in the hook must be true and verified. Numbers must match the clip.

Return the 3 options and mark your pick.
